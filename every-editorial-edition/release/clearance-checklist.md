# Clearance Checklist

Before adding or distributing editorial-edition content:

- Confirm the subscriber access and packaging decision.
- Verify each guide is intended for external subscriber use.
- Remove or generalize internal-only process references.
- Exclude personal strategy, analytics, and private source material.
- Confirm quoted or reproduced writing examples are cleared.
- Verify both Claude and Codex manifests reflect the edition accurately.
