# VOICE.md

This file does not describe your voice. Your voice comes from the gold examples in `examples/`: Compound Writing reads them before it drafts or edits and works out the sentence- and word-level patterns from your own writing.

This file collects **lessons**: corrections on wording, sentence construction, punctuation, or tone that the examples alone did not teach. A lesson wins when it conflicts with a pattern found in the examples.

## Scope

- **Writer or voice:** [Name]
- **Applies to:** [Your writing generally, a publication, or a format]
- **Examples used:** `examples/` [or name another folder or a subset for this scope]

## What counts as a lesson

- You corrected a draft that had already been matched to your examples, or you stated a sentence-level preference directly.
- It changes wording, sentence construction, punctuation, or tone. Argument, evidence, structure, and readiness rules go in `STYLE.md`.
- It is specific enough to change a sentence.

Do not add adjectives about the voice, a general portrait, or patterns the examples already show. If the examples would show it with one more piece, add the piece to `examples/` instead.
