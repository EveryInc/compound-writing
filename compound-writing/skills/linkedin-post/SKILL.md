# LinkedIn Post

## Context

Load `voice.md` and `style-guides/linkedin.md`. Load strategy files for
positioning, platform selection, repurposing, or performance analysis.

## Focus

- Open through a professional moment, tension, responsibility, or changed
  understanding of the work.
- Include builder or practitioner evidence when the insight depends on it.
- End with a useful implication, not generic encouragement.
- Use humor as brief recognition or release when it preserves credibility.
