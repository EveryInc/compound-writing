# Longform

## Context

Load `voice.md`, `style-guides/longform.md`, piece-specific sources, and
current `context.md` when available. Use strategy only when the assignment
includes positioning or cross-platform reuse.

## Focus

- Develop scenes, examples, evidence, argument, and payoff at longform scale.
- Preserve sentence-level voice while letting structure serve the piece.
- Keep claims traceable and clearly distinguish interpretation.
- Use humor only where it sharpens or releases pressure without trivializing
  the subject.
