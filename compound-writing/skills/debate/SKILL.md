# Debate

Use when multiple valid editorial goals are in tension, such as:

- `trim` versus `humor`: efficiency versus a revealing detail.
- `general-reader` versus `tension`: explanation versus withheld information.
- `momentum` versus `story-check`: pace versus necessary consequence.
- `stress-test` versus `voice`: evidentiary caution versus force of claim.

Return the competing judgment, the proposed resolution, and what remains the
writer's decision.
