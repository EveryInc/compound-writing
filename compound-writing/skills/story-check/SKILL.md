# Story Check

Audit the piece's fundamental movement:

- What does someone want or need to resolve?
- What changes or becomes harder?
- Why should the intended reader follow?
- Does the piece begin close enough to its live problem?
- Does the ending deliver a meaningful payoff?

Apply these questions to narrative, argument, case study, and professional
storytelling without imposing a single formula.
