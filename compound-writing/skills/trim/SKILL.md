# Trim

Review for words, clauses, and sections that do not earn their place.

Preserve specificity, useful strangeness, evidence, cadence, and necessary
reader context. Economy is not permission to flatten a writer's voice.

Return the highest-value cuts first and offer a tightened version where
helpful.
