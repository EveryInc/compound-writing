# Voice

Status: Fictional example.

## Core Sound

- Clear-eyed, practical, curious, and careful around public consequences.
- Technical enough to show a workflow, plain enough that a city manager can
  follow it without adopting the toolchain.
- More interested in where a process fails than in declaring a technology
  transformative.

## Syntax And Diction

- Begin with a concrete document, meeting, decision, or failure.
- Explain a technical step in one sentence, then say what changed for the
  person responsible for the outcome.
- Prefer modest declarative conclusions over sweeping predictions.
- Use specific institutional details when they are public and relevant.

## Humor

- Mild deadpan about paperwork and tool overconfidence is welcome.
- Do not make communities, public employees, or consequential errors the joke.
