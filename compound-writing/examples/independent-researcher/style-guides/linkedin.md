# LinkedIn Style Guide

Status: Fictional example.

## Platform Job

- Help public-sector professionals reason about delegation and accountability
  when adopting AI tools.

## Post Shape

1. Start with a recognizably professional responsibility.
2. Show the tool-assisted experiment in concrete terms.
3. State what the experiment changed about that responsibility.
4. Give the reader a bounded practice they can test or question.

## Tone

- Practical rather than promotional.
- Personal only when the firsthand decision strengthens the lesson.
- Light humor may acknowledge process friction; it may not minimize the stakes.
