# Longform Style Guide

Status: Fictional example.

## Destination Job

Document a public-sector workflow experiment in enough detail that a
practitioner can understand its method, correction points, and limits.

## Shape

1. Begin with the actual administrative task and why accuracy is consequential.
2. Show the tool-assisted method and the source material.
3. Make failures and human corrections visible.
4. End with a bounded takeaway another team could test responsibly.

## Voice And Evidence

- Keep the prose direct and specific.
- Include public source links and correction logs.
- Use mild deadpan only around process friction, never around affected people
  or public consequences.
