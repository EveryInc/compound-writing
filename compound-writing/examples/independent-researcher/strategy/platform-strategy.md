# Platform Strategy

Status: Fictional example for Alex Rivera.

## Core Work Unit

Each cycle begins with a public administrative task and a documented test.
For the current example, Alex turns a public hearing transcript into a
briefing, then records where human review corrected or qualified the result.

## Platform Roles

| Destination | Audience | Job | Entry point | Useful evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Longform | Researchers and implementers | Explain the test, errors, and review method in full. | The transcript-to-briefing experiment. | Workflow, correction log, source links. |
| X | Civic technology practitioners | Make the build and correction inspectable quickly. | One corrected claim from the generated briefing. | Annotated screenshot and source document. |
| LinkedIn | Public-sector operators and managers | Translate the experiment into professional responsibility. | Who signs off on a briefing after a tool drafts it. | Review checklist and bounded lesson. |

## Repurposing Rule

The artifact and verified finding remain constant. The entry point changes:
X begins with the receipt; LinkedIn begins with responsibility; longform shows
the complete test and its limitations.

## Measurement And Learning

| Destination | Signals worth observing | What the system may suggest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X | Saves, substantive replies, artifact clicks. | Whether receipt-first openings are a durable platform rule. |
| LinkedIn | Practitioner discussion and requests for the checklist. | Whether accountability checklists are a repeatable professional doorway. |
| Longform | Citations, replies about method, or adoption questions. | Whether a failure-log section belongs in every case study. |

No signal updates strategy automatically. Alex reviews the evidence and
approves any change to these rules.
