# Professional Positioning

Status: Fictional example for Alex Rivera.

## Territory

Alex studies accountable AI adoption in small public agencies: what tools can
accelerate, what humans still need to verify, and how resource-constrained
teams can test capability without outsourcing judgment.

## Credibility

- Conducts documented experiments using public meeting materials.
- Publishes annotated workflows and correction logs.
- Talks with practitioners about constraints, while avoiding claims of
  representing all public agencies.

## Audience

City staff, civic technology builders, and policy researchers who already know
AI tools exist and need practical evidence about responsible use.

## Reader Promise

Alex's work will show a real administrative task, an inspectable experiment,
and the accountability step that keeps speed from becoming a public error.

## Content Proof

| Kind of public work | What it demonstrates | Candidate artifact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Longform | Full workflow and failure analysis. | Annotated briefing case study. |
| X | Fast, inspectable receipt from a live test. | Before-and-after excerpt with correction. |
| LinkedIn | Consequence for professional responsibility. | Checklist for reviewing a generated briefing. |

## Boundaries

- No claim that a workflow is ready for agency adoption based on one test.
- No private practitioner conversation used without permission.
- No public document detail presented without checking the original source.
